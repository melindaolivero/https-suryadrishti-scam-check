# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Goal
Help seniors safely identify scams and make informed decisions using AI.

---

## Target Users
- Seniors (primary)
- Family caregivers (secondary)

---

## Problem
Seniors are frequently targeted by scams involving urgency, impersonation, and emotional manipulation. Existing tools are not simple or reassuring enough.

---

## MVP Scope
- Voice input
- Text input
- AI scam analysis
- Risk explanation
- Recommended actions

---

## Out of Scope
- Deepfake video detection
- Real-time call interception
- Financial integrations

---

## Success Metrics
- User understanding of scam risk
- Clarity of AI explanation
- User confidence after interaction
- Reduction in unsafe actions (theoretical metric)

---

## Key Tradeoffs
- False positives vs user safety
- Simplicity vs feature richness
- Automation vs user control

---

## UX Principles
- Calm tone
- Large readable UI
- No fear-based messaging
- Clear next steps
